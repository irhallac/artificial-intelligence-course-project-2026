Grading

The project score is out of 100. Its conversion to course bonus points will be discussed and announced in class.

Area Points What is expected
Research question and experiment design 20 A clear question; conditions that answer it; the same base cases across conditions; no unnecessary factors changed together.
Test set and answer program 25 Valid cases; answers produced or checked by the program; matched Turkish and English versions; fixed answer labels reasonably balanced; code tested on examples.
Experiment and reproducibility 20 Exact models, versions, prompts, settings, and scoring rule recorded; all planned cases run; raw responses kept.
Results and error analysis 25 Correct/total counts and accuracy; conditions compared correctly; invalid answers reported; repeated errors examined; claims supported by results.
README and video 10 A complete and readable README; stated limitations; a 5–10 minute video in which every group member appears and explains part of the work.
Total 100

The optional improvement study is not required for full credit. A submission without the required video is incomplete.
